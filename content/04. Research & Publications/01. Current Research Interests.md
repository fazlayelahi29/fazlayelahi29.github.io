- **Current Research Interests & Foundations**
    
      
    - **Research Overview & Strategic Objectives:** My primary focus encompasses semiconductor device modeling, compact modeling, and SPICE modeling, extending deeply into carrier transport, electrothermal behavior, and reliability physics (TDDB, HCI, NBTI). The scope targets nanoscale architectures including FinFET, GAAFET, RibbonFET, Forksheet FET, Nanosheet FET, and post-CMOS devices (NCFET, FeFET, TFET, CNTFET). It expands into wide and ultra-wide bandgap materials ($GaN$, $SiC$, $Ga_2O_3$, $AlN$, Diamond) specifically engineered for High-Electron-Mobility Transistor (HEMT) structures. My technical foundations span across Technology Computer-Aided Design (TCAD), Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (ASIC), Field Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGA), Analog ICs, and VLSI design.
        
          
        
    - **Research Journey & Contextual Story:** My current specialization in semiconductor modeling is not an isolated endeavor; rather, it is the natural, evolutionary culmination of diverse hardware investigations. A significant portion of my past research experiences directly form the foundation for my current research interests. By transitioning through systems-level macro-infrastructure, photonics, computational materials, and rigid digital logic frameworks, I cultivated the specific analytical resilience required for atomic-level physical modeling.
        
          
        
    - **Showcase & Achievements:** Currently preparing comprehensive device modeling research for formal peer review and journal submission, actively expanding a portfolio of academic posters and technical articles.
        
          
        
    - **Technical Tools & Software Utilized:** Synopsys Sentaurus TCAD, EDA Toolchains, Custom Obsidian Academic Stylesheets for IEEE Dual-Column PDF Generation.
