## Project 4: Dynamic Maximum Power Transfer Optimization via Parametric Sweeps

- The Idea & Circuit Structure: Connect an independent DC voltage source ($V_1 = 12\text{V}$) with an internal source resistance ($R_s = 500\Omega$) directly to a variable load resistor labeled as $R_L$.
- Mathematical Hand Calculations: Write down the algebraic equation for power dissipation across the load: $P_L = I^2 \times R_L = [V_1 / (R_s + R_L)]^2 \times R_L$. Differentiate $P_L$ with respect to $R_L$, set the derivative to zero, and mathematically prove that the maximum power is delivered to the load only when $R_L = R_s = 500\Omega$. Calculate the peak theoretical power value.

Open the waveform plot window, click on the load resistor while holding the `Alt` key to plot active power consumption ($V \times I$).
- What to Document in Your Google Doc: Plot the resulting parabolic power curve against the swept resistance value of $R_L$. Mark the peak of the parabola using the LTspice cursor tool to show that it occurs precisely at $500\Omega$.
