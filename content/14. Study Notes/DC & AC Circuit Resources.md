## Free Web Courses & Textbooks

### MIT OpenCourseWare: Circuits and Electronics (6.002)

**URL:** [ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/)

  

> [!success] Course Highlights
> 
> Covers lumped circuit abstraction, DC resistive networks, energy-storage elements (capacitors/inductors), RC/RL/RLC transient response, and sinusoidal AC steady-state analysis. Includes downloadable lecture notes and lab guides.
> 
>   

### All About Circuits: Lessons in Electric Circuits

**URL:** [allaboutcircuits.com/textbook](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/)

A complete open-source textbook series by Tony R. Kuphaldt:

  

- **Volume I (DC):** Basic electrical parameters, Ohm's law, series/parallel networks, Kirchhoff's laws, and network theorems.
    
      
    
- **Volume II (AC):** Basic AC theory, complex numbers, capacitive/inductive reactance, Impedance, resonance, and three-phase AC.
    
      
    

### Khan Academy & Engineer4Free

- **Khan Academy:** [EE Circuit Analysis](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/electrical-engineering/ee-circuit-analysis-topic) — Step-by-step walkthroughs of basic circuit elements, Nodal Analysis, mesh current methods, Thevenin/Norton equivalents, and phasors.
    
      
    
- **Engineer4Free:** [Electrical Circuits Course](https://www.engineer4free.com/circuits.html) — Over 50 concise video lessons covering foundational DC circuit reduction, voltage/current divider rules, and power calculations.
    
      
    

### NPTEL (Swayam): Basic Electric Circuits

**URL:** [onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/noc22_ee93](https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/noc22_ee93/preview)

  

> [!abstract] Academic Rigor
> 
> University-level lectures prepared by IIT faculty detailing rigorous circuit theory, matrix methods, transient behavior, and AC phasor analysis.
> 
>

## Interactive Circuit Simulators

> [!info] Hands-On Practice
> 
> An expanded collection of open-access textbooks, interactive circuit simulators, and structured video series provides additional tools for mastering DC and AC circuit theory.
> 
>   

_Figure 1: Example of an AC frequency sweep (Bode plot) modeled in SPICE._

  

- **Falstad Circuit Simulator:** [falstad.com/circuit](https://www.falstad.com/circuit/) — A free browser-based visual tool that animates current flow and voltage drops in real time. It includes pre-built examples for DC resistor networks, RC/RL transient charging, and AC RLC resonance circuits.
    
      
    
- **LTspice:** [Analog Devices LTspice](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/design-tools-and-calculators/ltspice-simulator.html) — The standard open-access SPICE simulation software used in engineering coursework for DC operating point calculations, AC frequency sweeps, and transient analysis.
    
      
    
- **Multisim Live:** [multisim.com](https://www.multisim.com/) — A browser-based circuit editor by National Instruments that lets you construct schematics and probe AC/DC voltages using virtual oscilloscopes and multimeters.
    
      
    

## Open Textbooks & Practice Repositories

- **[DC and AC Circuits Textbooks (Dr. Chad Davis)](https://ecstudiosystems.com/books.html)**: Openly licensed engineering textbooks covering DC network reduction, mesh/nodal matrix equations, first/second-order transient responses, and complex impedance.
    
      
    
- **[Socratic Electronics (Tony R. Kuphaldt)](https://www.ibiblio.org/kuphaldt/socrates/)**: An extensive open-access archive of diagnostic worksheets, quantitative practice problems, and step-by-step circuit solutions for engineering students.
    
      
    
- **[Circuit Theory Reference (WikiBooks)](https://freecomputerbooks.com/Circuit-Theory.html)**: A peer-reviewed open manual aligned with first-year electrical and computer engineering core curricula.
